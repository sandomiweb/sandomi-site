Our story

A sweet journey
that began in Guria

From a family bakery to fully automated confectionery production. A story of Georgian sweets made with love.

  • 2001

    Beginning in Ozurgeti

    “Vasadze’s Bread” starts with bread and bakery products.

  • 2011

    Sandomi is born

    Confectionery production and a new brand open in Makvaneti village.

  • 2013

    Second plant opens in Tbilisi

    A larger, large-scale confectionery plant opened in Tbilisi as well.

  • 2016

    Two plants: Tbilisi and Ozurgeti

    Production expands; products in 9,000+ stores across Georgia.

  • 2018

    ISO 22000 and HACCP

    International quality and safety standards.

  • 2019

    Golden Brand

    Consistent leader in bakery production.

  • 2020

    European technology

    Plant fully equipped and automated.

  • 2025

    On the world market

    Gulfood Dubai, Shanghai, Caspian Agro; exports to the USA and Palestine.

  • 2026

    25 years · SIAL Paris

    Anniversary and participation in SIAL Paris 2026.
